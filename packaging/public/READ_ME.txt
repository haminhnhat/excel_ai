EXCEL AI CONTROLLER - PUBLIC PORTABLE (WINDOWS X64)

1. Giai nen TOAN BO ZIP vao thu muc co quyen ghi, vi du Documents.
2. Mo "Excel AI Controller.bat". Trinh duyet se tu mo khi app san sang.
3. Trong "Setup new Excel file", chon file .xlsx/.xlsm cua ban, bam Analyze.
4. Kiem tra cac o input/output va mapping duoc de xuat; sua neu can.
5. Bam Validate selection, sau do Create profile.
6. Chuyen sang Run scenario de chay voi workbook va profile vua tao.

Khong can cai Python, pip hay thu vien. Khong can quyen Administrator.
Can Windows 10/11 64-bit va Microsoft Excel desktop da kich hoat de tinh
lai cong thuc. Excel Online khong thay the duoc Excel desktop.

Ban public KHONG kem workbook, mapping/profile, log hay du lieu khach hang.
Mapping tu dong chi la de xuat: luon kiem tra truoc khi chay Excel.
Sau khi dong/mo lai app, chon profile da luu va chon lai workbook o Run
scenario; khong can tao lai mapping neu cau truc workbook khong thay doi.

Giu cua so app mo khi su dung. Nhan Ctrl+C de dung app khi hoan tat.
Dong tab trinh duyet khong tu dung server.
Khong mo app ben trong ZIP, khong dat thu muc app trong Program Files.
Neu cong 8000 dang ban, app tu chon cong tiep theo (URL trong cua so app).

Che do mac dinh: phan tich lenh cuc bo (AI_PROVIDER=mock), khong can API key
hay Internet; day khong phai LLM online. Server chi mo tren 127.0.0.1.
File upload nam trong uploads, mapping trong config/profiles, ket qua
trong outputs va nhat ky trong logs. Cac du lieu nay nam tren may cua ban.
Khong chia se lai thu muc da su dung: no co the chua du lieu rieng.
De phan phoi, chi gui file ZIP public nguyen goc.

Neu Windows/antivirus canh bao, xac minh nguon tai; khong tat antivirus.
Goi nay chua duoc ky so. Neu loi, gui thong tin loi cho nguoi ho tro;
kiem tra va loai du lieu nhay cam truoc khi chia se log.

Phien ban: build-info.json, requirements-portable.txt.
Giay phep Python: runtime/LICENSE.txt. Giay phep thu vien: cac thu muc
runtime/Lib/site-packages/*.dist-info va cac file license tuong ung.
